# 《为彼而去》文本（未知图腾 · 阿露福遗迹 · 神都遗迹）

**状态：草稿第 2 版（2026-10-03）**：主线已定，按新结构重写；分段与旁白待用户确认。

标注【旁】为制作者根据设定撰写的旁白，【官】为官方原文。
心金魂银没有官方中文，阿露福一族与神都遗迹的内容全部是【旁】：中文是我们写的，括号里注明依据的游戏原文（英文取自 pret/pokeheartgold 反编译文本 `files/msgdata/msg/*.gmm`，即游戏官方英文；地面文字取自 Bulbapedia「Ruins of Alph」，日文取自 52poke「阿露福遗迹」「未知图腾笔记（道具）」）。英文字幕在依据原文的地方尽量贴近原文措辞。

## 核实过的原文

### 图鉴（官方中文）
- 分类：象征宝可梦 / Symbol Pokémon，No.0201（PokeAPI）。
- 晶灿钻石：很像古代文明的文字。是先有文字还是先有未知图腾，这是世界七大不可思议之一。 / Because the Pokémon is shaped like ancient writing, it is a huge mystery whether written language or Unown came first.
- 明亮珍珠：据说它们会用心灵感应与伙伴互通想法。平时总是贴在墙上。 / They seem to communicate among each other telepathically. They are always found stuck on walls.
- 阿尔宙斯：奇形怪状不似生物，且个体之间形状各异，本人指出其身姿与异国文字的共通之处，但未获重视。 / It is hard to believe these strangely shaped Pokémon are truly living creatures. I've pointed out that the species' many forms resemble writing from other lands; no one will take me seriously.

### 随意遗迹石板（《阿尔宙斯》，官方中文）
- 所有的生命皆会与其他生命相遇，并让新事物诞生。 / All lives touch other lives to create something anew and alive
  （英文是钻石·珍珠石室刻文；动画 DP060 用过这块石板，但讲的是另一个故事）

### 阿露福遗迹地面文字（心金魂银，用未知图腾文字拼写；无官方中文）

| 化石翼龙 | Our clan created Pokémon statue outside | わたしたち そとの ポケモン ぞう つくる | Thus we shall erect a Pokémon statue outside |

- 入口那句研究员也说"还不知道是什么意思"，片中只能当作未解之谜，不做解释。

### 研究员与《未知图腾笔记》（心金魂银）
- 研究员（解开全部谜题后）：It seems that UNOWN were living at the ruins with a human clan. It is thought that this clan made the Pokémon statues underground. The UNOWN were easily scared and wouldn't go outside. When more and more people started to appear around the ruins, the clan that wished to live in harmony with the UNOWN eventually left...
- 笔记第 9 页英文写成 "relatives of humans"，是误译：日文是「アンノーンと にんげんの いちぞくが すんでいた」（未知图腾和人类的一族住在这里）。以研究员台词和日文为准，写成"一族"。
- 笔记第 4 页：That puzzle was like a key somehow. It woke the UNOWN up from their sleep.
- 遗迹里的人：The ruins have not been robbed, nor tampered with. No graffiti, either. Things are well preserved. Something here must make people feel they should preserve it.

### 神都遗迹（心金魂银·阿尔宙斯活动）
- 研究员（带阿尔宙斯进入遗迹时）：Hmm. I have been studying the ruins for many years, but something feels... different. / Wow! The UNOWN pictures on the wall... It feels like they're staring at me!
- 研究员（回来后）：The power of ARCEUS and UNOWN affected each other to create a huge energy, which sent you to the Sinjoh Ruins...? UNOWN, the Ruins of Alph, and ARCEUS... The mystery deepens...
- 竹兰：A long time ago...people came from Sinnoh to live here. They must have built a temple here while longing for home.
- 竹兰：What is now the Sinjoh Ruins was built to honor ARCEUS by the Sinnoh people... It's an icebound temple that has been waiting for ARCEUS for hundreds of years...
- 登山男：The ancient ruins here have inherited the legends from both the Ruins of Alph in Johto and the Spear Pillar in Sinnoh. That is proof that people from Johto and Sinnoh blended together as a group.
- 登山男：When people moved from place to place, the myths and legends of Pokémon and their power would also be carried with those people.
- 竹兰：According to an ancient document, time, space, and antimatter, or what combined we call the world, shall be born when ARCEUS stands on the Mystri Stage.
- 竹兰：Depicted on the Mystri Stage are the Pokémon that shaped this world. The circle in the middle is ARCEUS, the origin of it all...
- 竹兰（蛋出现时）：That shining sphere... Could it be...an Egg? Did we just witness the very moment an Egg was brought to this world?
- 竹兰：An Egg is the cradle of every being. This planet itself is an Egg in a sense... Life that comes from an Egg will come to an end in due course... to begin anew... That may be what ARCEUS wanted to show us.
- 游戏画面：阿尔宙斯站上三幻舞台中央，被一圈未知图腾环绕 → 宇宙 → 阿尔宙斯 → 地球 → 舞台图案亮起 → 一颗蛋出现并裂开 → 光球化作帝牙卢卡 / 帕路奇亚 / 骑拉帝纳之一（原作的实景照片蒙太奇不用）。

### 不能用的说法
- **"阿露福一族去了神都"**：游戏里没有。只能说神都遗迹"继承了阿露福遗迹和枪之柱两边的传说"，一族去了哪里，游戏没说。
- "电波的旋律就是天界之笛"：Bulbapedia 的说法，不是游戏原文。
- 水晶日文版里"电波塔建成后才出现图案"一说：英文版删掉了，和笔记矛盾，不用。
- 动画设定：剧场版第 3 部《结晶塔的帝王》里未知图腾能把愿望变成现实；动画里有"未知图腾的次元"；这些游戏里都没有。TV 动画金银篇的阿露福遗迹（EP163）是化石发掘地，没有未知图腾。

### 名称（待核实官方中文用例）
阿露福遗迹（アルフのいせき）、神都遗迹（シントいせき）、三幻舞台（みつぶたい）都只在心金魂银出现，没有官方中文；现用 52poke 的叫法，港台译名一致。竹兰、枪之柱、帝牙卢卡、帕路奇亚、骑拉帝纳、阿尔宙斯为官方中文。


## 主线（用户 2026-10-03 定）
**一族人爱着未知图腾，爱到选择离开；他们留下的文字替他们留了下来，也把故事带到了更远的地方。**
主题：守护，有时是退后一步；人会离开，话会留下。与《看不见的信任》互为镜像（那一期是宝可梦因人的恐惧而离开人，这一期是人因爱而离开宝可梦）。
观众全程只跟一个问题："他们为什么要走？"——前半回答"为什么"，后半回答"走了之后呢"。
- 拼图、电波、四间石室等机制只留"拼图像一把钥匙"；竹兰、枪之柱等专有名词能不提就不提。
- 随意遗迹那句"所有的生命……"不用（与主线无关）。
- 给资深观众的一层，每处只一两句：四幅拼图"也许"是刻给未知图腾看的外面世界；入口那句没人读懂的话；神都遗迹是配信阿尔宙斯才能触发的隐藏剧情；三幻舞台的时间、空间、反物质接回第 1 期《起始之物》。

## 分段草稿（约 2 分 30 秒，待定稿）

### ① 字（约 18 秒，钩子）
- 漆黑的石室，火把光扫过墙上的刻文；刻文一只接一只睁开白色的眼睛——是未知图腾。显色峰值放叫声，名称卡：No.0201 · 象征宝可梦 / SYMBOL POKÉMON · 未知图腾 / UNOWN。
- 【官·晶灿钻石】很像古代文明的文字。是先有文字还是先有未知图腾，这是世界七大不可思议之一。 / Because the Pokémon is shaped like ancient writing, it is a huge mystery whether written language or Unown came first.
- 【旁】城都的阿露福遗迹，是它们的家。 / The Ruins of Alph in Johto are their home.

### ② 一族（约 25 秒）
- 地面上的未知图腾文字逐句亮起金光（用未知图腾立绘排出英文原句）；火把光点与长影子表示人，不画人形；石像在火光里浮现。
- 【旁】很久以前，有一族人和未知图腾一起住在这里。 / Long ago, a human clan lived here together with the Unown.
  （研究员：It seems that UNOWN were living at the ruins with a human clan.）
- 【旁】他们在地上刻下文字：「吾等一族之言，刻于此地。」 / They carved words into the floor: "Our clan engraved words in this place."
- 【旁】还为它们造了宝可梦的石像。 / They also made Pokémon statues for them.
  （化石翼龙石室：Our clan created Pokémon statue outside；研究员：this clan made the Pokémon statues underground）
- 【旁】石室的拼图上，刻着化石盔、菊石兽、化石翼龙和凤王。 / The stone puzzles show Kabuto, Omanyte, Aerodactyl, and Ho-Oh.
- 【旁】也许，那是他们刻给未知图腾看的，外面的世界。 / Perhaps they were the outside world, carved for the Unown to see.
  （**推测**，用户 2026-10-03 同意以"也许"呈现；依据：日文「そとの ポケモン ぞう つくる」+ 下一句未知图腾拒绝外界）

### ③ 为彼而去（约 30 秒，情绪落点）
- 【旁】可未知图腾能察觉人的意识，却拒绝外面的世界。 / But the Unown could sense the minds of others, and they rejected the outside world.
  （菊石兽石室：They possess power to sense with mind and reject outside）
- 【旁】它们非常胆小，从不肯走出遗迹。 / They were timid, and would never leave the ruins.
  （研究员：The UNOWN were easily scared and wouldn't go outside.）
- 遗迹外的火把越聚越多，逼近石室。
- 【旁】后来，遗迹附近的人越来越多。 / Then more and more people came to the ruins.
  （研究员：When more and more people started to appear around the ruins…）
- 【旁】于是，那一族人刻下了最后一句话—— / So the clan carved one last message—
- 【旁】「吾等人类，当与彼同行。为彼，吾等启程。」 / "We humans need to walk with them. We leave here for the sake of them."
  （凤王石室；全片标题出处）
- 一族的火把一支支远去、熄灭；未知图腾贴回墙上，闭上眼睛。

### ④ 话留下了（约 22 秒）
- 【旁】他们走了，遗迹却一直完好。 / They were gone, but the ruins remained untouched.
- 【旁】没有被盗掘，也没有人在墙上留下涂鸦。好像有什么，让来到这里的人都想守护它。 / Never robbed, never defaced—as if something here makes people want to protect it.
  （遗迹里的人：The ruins have not been robbed, nor tampered with. No graffiti, either. … Something here must make people feel they should preserve it.）
- 拼图石板归位、地面塌陷、成群的未知图腾同时醒来（**爆发点一**，猛推 + 震动）。
- 【旁】直到有一天，石室的拼图被解开。它像一把钥匙，唤醒了沉睡的未知图腾。 / Until one day, the puzzles were solved. Like a key, they woke the Unown from their sleep.
  （笔记第 4 页：That puzzle was like a key somehow. It woke the UNOWN up from their sleep.）
- 【旁】入口的地上，还刻着一句至今没人读懂的话： / By the entrance is one more line that no one has understood:
- 【旁·半文言译】「同伴仰望，祝福吾等。」 / "Our friends look up and heartily congratulate us."（入口小房间地面文字原文）
- 成片（2026-10-03）实际字幕：他们走了，遗迹却一直完好。 / The clan was gone, yet the ruins remained intact.｜没有被盗掘，也没有人在墙上留下涂鸦。 / They were never robbed or tampered with. ### ⑤ 故事走得更远（约 25 秒）
- 墙上所有的眼睛同时转向镜头 → 推进眼白，白色铺满画面 → 遥远北方的雪原，雪墙环绕的冰封神殿。
- 【旁】人们迁徙时，宝可梦的神话也随人一起流传。 / When people moved from place to place, the myths of Pokémon traveled with them.
  （登山男：When people moved from place to place, the myths and legends of Pokémon and their power would also be carried with those people.）
- 【旁】在遥远的北方，有一座被冰雪封住的神殿。 / Far to the north stands a temple sealed in snow and ice.
  （竹兰：an icebound temple；地图说明：A snow-covered area of old legends, surrounded by a wall of snow.）
- 【旁】它继承了阿露福遗迹的传说。 / It inherited the legends of the Ruins of Alph.
  （登山男：The ancient ruins here have inherited the legends from both the Ruins of Alph in Johto and the Spear Pillar in Sinnoh.）
- 【旁】那一族人后来去了哪里，没有人知道。 / Where the clan went, no one knows.
- 【旁】可他们的故事，走得比他们更远。 / But their story traveled farther than they did.
- 成片（2026-10-03）：英文第 1 句改为 When people moved from place to place, the myths of Pokémon traveled with them.；"遥远的北方"依据 Bulbapedia：宝可梦装置的地图指向城都遥远的北方（Location: North of Johto）。

### ⑥ 他们守护的是什么（约 25 秒，全片最大爆发点）
- 阿尔宙斯在神殿中央显色 + 叫声 + 名称卡（分类官方中文待核实）。
- 【旁】这座神殿，是为阿尔宙斯而建的。 / This temple was built to honor Arceus.
  （竹兰：The Sinjoh Ruins are what remains of a temple built to show respect for ARCEUS.）
- 阿尔宙斯站上三幻舞台，从不肯离开遗迹的未知图腾成群涌来，环绕成黑色光环；天空裂开成宇宙 → 地球 → 舞台图案亮起 → 一颗蛋出现、裂开。阿尔宙斯第二次叫声（**爆发点二**）。
- 【旁】从不肯离开遗迹的未知图腾，在这里环绕着阿尔宙斯。 / Here, the Unown that never left their ruins gathered around Arceus.
  （游戏画面；研究员：The power of ARCEUS and UNOWN affected each other to create a huge energy）
- 【旁】古老的文献记载：阿尔宙斯站上这座舞台时，时间、空间与反物质——也就是世界，将会诞生。 / An ancient text says when Arceus stands on this stage, time, space, and antimatter—what we call the world—shall be born.
  （竹兰原台词；接回第 1 期）
- 【旁】未知图腾、阿露福遗迹、阿尔宙斯……谜团越来越深。 / The Unown, the Ruins of Alph, and Arceus… The mystery only deepens.
  （研究员原台词；只提问，不下结论）

### ⑦ 话还在（约 15 秒）
- 回到阿露福石室，金光渐暗，地面那第一句文字最后一次亮起。
- 【旁】一族人走了。 / The clan is gone.
- 【旁】可他们刻下的话，还在这里，陪着未知图腾。 / But the words they carved are still here, keeping the Unown company.
